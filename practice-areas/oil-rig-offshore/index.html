Houston Offshore and Oil Rig Injury Lawyer

Oil rig and offshore cases, built for trial

The sea has different rules.

Offshore injury cases live under the Jones Act and general maritime law, not Texas workers’ comp. The rules are specialized, the evidence is specialized, and the defendants know it.

I take oil rig and offshore cases in Houston and throughout Texas. Platform falls and crew-transfer injuries, under maritime law and the Jones Act that protect offshore workers. Every case I accept is built for trial.

What I handle

  • Rig blowouts
  • Deck injuries
  • Equipment failures
  • Maintenance incidents
  • Helicopter and crew transfers

How I build the case

  1. 01

    Status comes first.

    Whether you qualify as a Jones Act seaman decides which law applies, so I establish it early.

  2. 02

    The vessel and its records.

    Maintenance logs, crew records, and incident reports show whether the vessel or rig was unseaworthy.

  3. 03

    Maritime experts.

    Experts in offshore operations explain the standard of care to a jury that has never worked a rig.
