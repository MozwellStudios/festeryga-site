When a refinery fails, a family pays.

Refinery and petrochemical incidents are rarely accidents. They are failures of process safety, equipment maintenance, or operator training. The cases turn on documentation.

Texas Gulf Coast, petrochemical

How I build the case.

  1. 01

    I document the scene first.

    Site conditions, equipment, and witness accounts, secured before the plant changes them.

  2. 02

    Process safety records.

    Maintenance logs, operator training files, and OSHA and Chemical Safety Board reports show what the company knew.

  3. 03

    Engineers who can explain it.

    Mechanical and process safety experts walk a jury through how the failure happened and who allowed it.
