Houston 18-Wheeler and Truck Accident Lawyer

Commercial truck cases, built for trial

A truck is not a car.

An 18-wheeler collision case is a corporate case. Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations govern driver hours, maintenance, logs, and ECM data. The evidence lives in the truck.

I take commercial truck cases in Houston and throughout Texas. Regulatory violations, logbooks, and the evidence a carrier would rather lose, preserved before it disappears. Every case I accept is built for trial.

What I handle

  • Jackknife collisions
  • Rear-end crashes at highway speed
  • Driver fatigue
  • Maintenance failures
  • Improper loading

  1. 01
