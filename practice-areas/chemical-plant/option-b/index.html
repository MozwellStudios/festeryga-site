Chemical exposure lasts a lifetime.

Chemical plant incidents cause injuries that unfold over years: toxic exposure, respiratory damage, long-tail illness. The cases demand medical experts who can map the full future.

I take chemical plant cases in Houston and throughout Texas, and I build every one for trial.

What I handle

  • Process incidents
  • Long-term exposure
  • Contractor incidents

How I build the case

  1. 1

    Map the exposure.

    What was released, for how long, and who was there, documented before the records go quiet.

  2. 2

    Plant records and medicine.

    I subpoena plant records and work with toxicologists and occupational medicine doctors.

    The full future.

    Long-term damages experts show a jury what the exposure will cost over a lifetime.
