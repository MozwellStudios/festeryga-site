I take the cases other firms pass on.

Refinery blasts, oil rig injuries, truck collisions: the ones where a family's future is on the line.

Case types

  • Personal injurySerious injuries caused by someone else's negligence, on the road, at work, or on someone's property.
  • Refinery and petrochemical incidentsBlasts, burns, and toxic exposure that trace back to safety shortcuts and cost-cutting on the plant floor.
  • Oil rig and offshore accidentsJones ActPlatform falls and crew-transfer injuries, under maritime law and the Jones Act that protect offshore workers.
  • Chemical plant accidentsReleases, explosions, and the chemical burns and respiratory injuries that follow.
  • Commercial truck collisionsFMCSARegulatory violations, logbooks, and the evidence a carrier would rather lose, preserved before it disappears.
  • Vehicle collisionsInjuries and deaths caused by negligent drivers: high-speed, impaired, commercial-fleet, and rideshare crashes.
  • Aviation accidentsPrivate, charter, and helicopter crashes, and the maintenance and pilot failures behind them.
  • Railroad accidentsFELACrossing collisions and rail-worker injuries, and the consequences that follow a family for years.
  • Industrial injuriesWorkplace negligence in aging plants and warehouses with inadequate safety protocols, from falls to equipment failures.
  • Product liabilityUnsafe products brought to market without proper testing: design defects, failures to warn, dangerous machinery.
  • Wrongful deathSurvival actionFamilies who lost someone to another's negligence.
  • Insurance disputesBad faithI confront insurers who deny legitimate claims, through the coverage and bad-faith litigation that forces payment.
  • Catastrophic injuriesCatastrophic injuries are defined by more than the pain they cause. They are defined by how they change a life, leaving it altered in some way forever.
