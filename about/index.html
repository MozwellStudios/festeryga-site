When I take your case, I lead it.

I'm Edward Festeryga. I started Festeryga Law because I wanted every client I represent to have my full attention.

Before this firm, I worked inside the machinery of high-volume injury practices. I saw what happens when a serious case is treated as inventory instead of a person's life. The client loses. Every time.

So when I accept your case, I personally lead the strategy, the negotiations, the preparation, and the litigation. You are not passed from intake to a case manager and left wondering who is responsible.

You get direct access to me, backed by a real team that keeps the work organized and the case moving, in English and Español.

If you hire me, you get me. That is the whole promise.

Mission

Healing Yesterday. Here Today. Bringing Hope To Tomorrow.

I provide justice to the people and communities that need it most. I fight for people facing tragic, unexpected, and often life-altering circumstances caused by someone else's negligence. When accidents happen, I bring relentless, state-of-the-art representation to hold corporations accountable and help people overcome what they never deserved.

Vision

Accidents don't just happen, and injured people need a champion. By holding corporations accountable for the harm they cause to people and their families, I work to make my community safer and to get justice for the people who deserve it. That means superior, relentless, efficient legal work, and making sure the people responsible answer for what they did.

Values

Justice

Accidents don't just happen. Almost always they could have been avoided, but a company cut corners on safety and put profit over people. Holding companies accountable and making communities safer is my guiding principle.

Community

Be good to others, and hold bullies accountable. Billion-dollar industries that only care about their bottom line need someone to face them. I fight every day so communities are protected and justice is served.

Integrity

Honesty, candor, and integrity are my foundation. Clear eyes and real experience with industries and corporations make for precise litigation that protects communities and gets my clients justice.

Innovation

Industries that harm people have resources. Beating them takes a lawyer with resources too: modern litigation technology, and the creativity to use it. That is the core of how I build a case.
