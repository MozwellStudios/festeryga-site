About Festeryga Law

I founded this firm in 2018 to work every case I accept as if my own family’s future depended on it. These are the people behind the practice.

When it’s everything.

I never back down.

Trial-tested. Client-focused. Result-proven.

I grew up in a farming community. My parents and grandparents, the children and grandchildren of European immigrants, taught me to be honest, to work hard, and to stand up to bullies. I still practice law that way.

I take on the companies that cut corners and the insurers that deny what they owe. Every case I accept is built for trial, so the other side has to deal with me.

Hockey away from home at 13. A walk-on spot on a national championship football team. Law school on scholarship. The harder the fight, the more I want it.

I’m all in.

When I take your case, you get my cell number and my full attention. I return your call myself, and I am the lawyer the jury sees.

To me, it’s personal.

The people I represent are not file numbers. They are families who lost a paycheck, a body that worked, or someone they love. I fight for them like they are mine.

Healing yesterday. Here today. Bringing hope to tomorrow.

Mission

I bring justice to the people and communities that need it most: people facing tragic, life-altering harm caused by someone else’s negligence. I hold the corporations responsible to account.

Vision

Injured people need a champion. Every company I hold accountable makes Houston a little safer, and every client I represent gets the justice they deserve.

Values

  • Justice

    Accidents almost never just happen. A company cut a corner and put profit over people. Holding it accountable makes the community safer.

  • Community

    Be good to others and hold bullies accountable. Billion-dollar industries need someone willing to face them.

  • Integrity

    Honesty and candor with my clients, the court, and the other side. Precise work starts with telling the truth.

  • Innovation

    Industries that hurt people have resources. I match them with modern litigation tools and the creativity to use them.
