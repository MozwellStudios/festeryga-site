01  ·  WHAT HAPPENED
I start with the specific facts. The industry, the equipment, the maintenance records, the people on shift that day. What actually failed. Who had the duty to prevent it. The case is built from the ground up, not fitted to a template.
02  ·  THE HARM CAUSED
Then the full picture of what was lost. The medical future. The lost income. The daily life that will never be the same. I work with the medical experts, the economists, the vocational specialists who can tell that story to a jury in a way they will remember.
03  ·  WHAT THE CASE REQUIRES
Finally, the strategy. Which defendants belong on the papers. Which insurer will be forced to sit at the table. Which deposition we take first and what we need from it. Which expert carries the credibility to break the defense's story. Every decision is made with the case walking into court, whether it goes there or not.
The right result starts with understanding the whole case.
