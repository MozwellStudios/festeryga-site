The cases behind the number

Trucks, plants, rigs and highways. Open any case to see how I built it.

  • Industrial injury2024

    $485,000

    Jury verdict for a plant worker crushed by a forklift.

    A forklift backed into my client on a night shift at a Beaumont plant. The company blamed him for standing in the lane. I subpoenaed the camera footage and the training logs. The operator was never certified. The jury put the fault on the company.

  • Commercial truck2025

    $410,000

    Settlement for a family rear-ended by an 18-wheeler on I-10.

    The driver had been on duty for 13 hours. The carrier offered $60,000 in the first month. I pulled the electronic logs, deposed the safety manager, and set the case for trial. They settled six weeks before jury selection.

  • Chemical plant2024

    $275,000

    Settlement for a contractor burned in a chemical release.

    A valve failed during a turnaround at a Pasadena plant and sprayed my client with caustic. The plant blamed its contractor. I proved the plant owned the valve, skipped the inspection, and kept the records that showed it.

  • Wrongful death2023

    $240,000

    Settlement for the family of a father killed at an intersection.

    A driver ran a stop sign in Sugar Land and killed a father of two on his way to work. The driver carried minimum coverage. I found a second policy through his employer and recovered for his wife and children.

  • Offshore injury2023

    $185,000

    Settlement for a deckhand injured under the Jones Act.

    A mooring line snapped and broke my client’s leg on a supply vessel off Galveston. The company cut off his maintenance and cure. I filed under the Jones Act and the payments started again within a month. The case settled before trial.

  • Product liability2022

    $150,000

    Settlement for a roofer hurt when his ladder failed.

    An extension ladder buckled under my client. He fell 14 feet and fractured his wrist and two vertebrae. I kept the ladder, hired an engineer, and showed the lock was defective. The manufacturer settled.

  • Vehicle collision2022

    $120,000

    Settlement for a rideshare passenger hit by a red-light runner.

    My client was in the back seat of a rideshare when a pickup ran a red light on Westheimer. Three insurers pointed at each other. I put all three on notice and recovered from each policy.

  • Insurance dispute2022

    $85,000

    Recovery after an insurer denied an underinsured motorist claim.

    My client’s own insurer denied her underinsured motorist claim, then offered $8,000. I sued for breach of contract and bad faith. They paid more than ten times the offer.

  • Vehicle collision2021

    $50,000

    Settlement for a motorcyclist cut off by a left turn.

    A driver turned left across my client’s lane on Washington Avenue. The insurer said a motorcycle was impossible to see. I used the intersection camera and a reconstruction expert. The insurer paid its policy limit.
