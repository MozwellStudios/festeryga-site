A spinal injury changes every day after it.

Spinal injuries come from car crashes, falls, and workplace accidents. The impact on the person and the family can be devastating, and the medical costs last a lifetime.

I represent people with spinal cord injuries in Houston and throughout Texas, and I build every case for trial.

What these cases involve

  • Complete and incomplete spinal cord injuries
  • Paralysis
  • Fractured and herniated discs
  • Injuries from falls
  • Injuries from vehicle crashes

How I build the case

  1. 1

    Medicine first.

    Treating surgeons and rehabilitation specialists document the injury and what recovery will realistically look like.

  2. 2

    A life care plan.

    Adaptive equipment, changes to the home, and ongoing care are priced by a life care planner.

  3. 3

    Lost earning capacity.

    Economists show a jury what the injury takes from a working life.
