Houston Burn Injury Lawyer

Burns heal slowly. Insurers move fast.

Thermal, chemical, and electrical burns. Reconstructive care over years, lasting physical limits, and the emotional weight that comes with them.

Free consultation(713) 414-4042

01 Human consequence

What changes

A burn can mean permanent physical limits, scarring, and heavy emotional trauma, made worse by medical debt and lost income. Insurers and employers often refuse to take burns seriously and deny or underpay the claim.

I represent people with burns in Houston and throughout Texas, and I build every case for trial.

  • Thermal burns
  • Chemical burns
  • Electrical burns
  • Explosion and fire injuries
  • Scarring and disfigurement
