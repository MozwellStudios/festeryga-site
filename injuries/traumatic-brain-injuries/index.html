A brain injury is hard to see and harder to prove.

From concussion to severe TBI. Changes to memory, thinking, and behavior that can need care for the rest of a life.

01 Human consequence

What changes

The brain is complex, and injuries to it are often hard to diagnose and treat. Some need care for the rest of a life. Going back to work is hard when memory or focus is gone.

I represent people with traumatic brain injuries in Houston and throughout Texas, and I build every case for trial.

  • Concussion and mild TBI
  • Moderate and severe TBI
  • Skull fractures
  • Brain injury from lack of oxygen
  • Changes to memory, focus, and behavior

02 Medical evidence

How I build the case.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis on the record.

    Imaging, neuropsychological testing, and treating doctors document an injury that is easy to miss.

  2. 02

    Who you were before.

    Family, coworkers, and friends show a jury who you were before the injury and what changed.

  3. 03

    The cost of a lifetime.

    Life care planners and economists put a number on future care and lost earning capacity.

03 Long-term damages

The whole cost.

An injury reshapes a life. The case has to account for all of it, and I am honest about what the law can and cannot restore.

  • Medical careTreatment so far, and the care still ahead.
  • Lost earningsWages lost, and the earning capacity the injury takes.
  • Pain and mental anguishWhat the injury costs a person, beyond the bills.
  • Impairment and disfigurementThe lasting physical change, and what it means for daily life.
