General

What does “no fee unless there is a recovery” mean?

You pay nothing upfront. If I don’t recover money for you, you owe no attorney fee. Read the full answer

Your case

How long will my case take?

It depends on the case. Simple cases where fault is clear can settle in weeks. More complex cases can take two to three years to resolve.

What if another firm already rejected my case?

Call anyway. I take cases other firms pass on, and the reason another firm said no may be the reason I say yes.
